# Data Safety Example

Safe handling rules:

- Secrets: never commit tokens, keys, credentials, or private environment files.
- Paths: use synthetic examples only; do not expose personal paths.
- Logs: redact sensitive data before sharing or committing.
- Music metadata: use synthetic metadata for tests and examples.
- Lyrics: do not store or reproduce lyrics.
- Fingerprints: do not commit audio fingerprints or derived private identifiers.
- Private data: minimize collection, redact outputs, and prefer fake fixtures.
