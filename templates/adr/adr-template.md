# ADR: Title

Use an ADR only when the **Design Gate** is active and the decision is architectural and meaningfully hard to reverse.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

## Context

What durable problem or constraint requires an architectural decision?

## Decision

What was chosen and why?

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, constrained, or newly required because of this decision?

## Alternatives

Only serious alternatives that clarify why the chosen direction matters.

## Reversal / Migration

How could this decision be replaced or rolled back later, and what would make that costly?
